Chris's words: *"You can see the loop coming down, into the entrance road, trimming it almost in half."*

**The law:** the descending leg stays clear of the entrance road along the WHOLE descent, not only at the touchdown point. The entrance road keeps its full width, untrimmed, at every station — no part of the descent overhanging it.

Chris's addition (2026-11-06 13:26): the trimming is not only an approach view — *"that eating of half the lane actually cuts the width of road in half during loop."* While driving the element itself, the other leg cuts the driver's usable width. The law therefore covers the whole element: at no point during the loop may either leg reduce the usable width of the leg being driven.
