## The measured envelope (every feature is sized from these)

- Top speed 48 m/s; honest racing pace 24–34 m/s.
- Lateral grip limit 11.0 m/s² → unbanked minimum radius at
  24 m/s ≈ 52 m; banked sweepers (bank supplies the rest)
  run R30–45 at 18–30 m/s (FP9/FP25 staged + run-verified).
- Flight: a slope-matched chasm crossing at 30 m/s flies
  ~1.2 s ≈ 36 m (ribbon gap, staged). Design gap band:
  20–35 m at approach speeds 26–34 m/s.
- Drumbeat band: 0.1–0.4 s hops need GENTLE humps on a flat
  base (FP21: the mule's descending row flies ~1.1 s — six
  times too much). Rebound: a big landing returns a second
  flight of ~½ the first (FP14, film-verified).
- Width 12.0 m (the ladder's settled answer); edge straddle
  full loss at 1.17 m overshoot (FP9).
- Drop Start: station air 1.70 m, fall 0.58 s (FP26).

## The laws

1. **NO REST SECTORS** (owner, 2026-10-30): every sector —
   straights included — carries undulations and incidentals
   demanding constant attention.
2. **Born with sections**: transverse section rhythm + dashed
   edges designed in from the first mesh (the mule's oldest
   wound, never retrofitted again). Section spacing sized to
   read at speed: ~15 m at racing pace (~2 per second).
3. **Earn/spend texture, not provinces**: payable air is
   distributed so no starved stretch exceeds ~500 m (the
   mule's was 2,000 m). Boost is spent on the straights —
   which are taxed by Law 1, so spending is never free.
4. **Honest sightlines**: near-field blindness is a defect;
   far-field blindness over crests is the difficulty engine
   and is designed deliberately (owner's dip doctrine).
5. **Crammond vocabulary first**: ramps, steep climbs and
   descents, banked corners, chasms, stepping stones,
   drawbridge. No loops (owner's nuke, 2026-10-30).
6. Every landing slope-matched by construction and verified
   by the feasibility method before the track is declared.

## Decisions

1. **Placement: STANDALONE** (owner, 2026-10-30) — prove the
   design first; season placement comes later.
2. **Setting: DESERT** (owner, 2026-10-30) — the existing
   world. Filed from the same exchange, NOT for this track:
   the gravity pondering — a moon track, a Mars track,
   gravity as the difference between worlds. In this engine
   gravity is one constant; flight, the crane, the suspension
   and the payout economy all re-derive from it, and the air
   audit would reprice every track automatically. An idea on
   file for later tracks, nothing more.

   working labels.
