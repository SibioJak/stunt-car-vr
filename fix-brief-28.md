Method decided by the owner (Jaklar), Fri 2026-10-30 19:26

assemble the track from what proves out." SCR's track shapes

## The instrument

A dedicated LAB ribbon (new track slot; circuit + ribbon
untouched): a straight-ish course stringing the candidate
pieces in sequence, each approached from a clean, flat,
pinned-state entry so every piece is judged from the same
state. Each piece carries a roadside number board (PIECE 1,
2, 3…) so the owner's verdicts map to pieces without
ambiguity. Pieces are independent: any one can be struck by
the owner's word without disturbing the others.

## The pieces (all sized from the measured envelope)

1. **Drumbeat rows ×3 gentlenesses** — flat-based hump rows,
   amplitudes bracketing the FP21 finding, to find by driving
   the hump this car loves (0.1–0.4 s band).
2. **The flair, formalised ×2 severities** — inside-edge lip
   on a banked sweeper; the discovered feature, at mild and
   at the run-18 strength.
3. **Crest launches ×3 steepnesses** — the tumble country:
   progressively sharper crests to find where the car starts
   wanting to go over (data for the flight-model question,
   not just track design).
4. **The measured chasm** — one slope-matched gap at the
   staged flight envelope (~28 m at ~30 m/s), landing
   verified by the feasibility method before it ships.
5. **Off-camber S** — flat, adverse-camber S at the grip
   limit (11 m/s² country): the no-air attention piece,
   Law 1's proof.
6. **Compression → rebound** — a dip-and-crest pair tuned to
   FP14's rebound: land, compress, second flight by design.
7. **Edge slalom** — markers forcing line choice along the
   edge-straddle zone: the straddle physics as gameplay.
8. **The taxed straight** — the Law-1 straight: swells at two
   amplitudes over one long straight, boost country that
   still demands hands.
